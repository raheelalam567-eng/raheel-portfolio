Yes. A review can identify the priority issues in structure, performance, design, or editing before we decide what needs rebuilding.

Will the website work on mobile?+

Responsive behavior is part of the build. We check navigation, content, forms, and important interactions across representative screen sizes.

Hosting and domain requirements can be discussed during scoping. They are separate decisions from the design and development work.
