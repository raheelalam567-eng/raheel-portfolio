03 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

Can you guarantee a return on ad spend?+

No. Performance depends on the offer, market, creative, budget, tracking, and landing-page experience. The work focuses on disciplined testing and clear measurement.

Do I need an existing ad account?+

If you have one, we can review it. If you are starting fresh, account setup and access requirements can be included in the agreed scope.

Is ad spend included in your fee?+

Media spend and service fees should be agreed separately. We define scope and budget before a campaign starts.
